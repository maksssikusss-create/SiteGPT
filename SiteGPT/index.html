Strengths and weaknesses

No assistant is perfect. Here is an honest summary.

Claude

Strengths

  • Natural, human-like writing
  • Very good at coding and long tasks
  • Handles very long documents
  • No ads on any plan

Weaknesses

  • Cannot create images
  • Free plan has tighter usage limits
  • Fewer extra features than ChatGPT

ChatGPT

Strengths

  • Many tools in one place
  • Image generation and strong voice mode
  • Cheap "Go" plan for $8
  • Huge user community and guides

Weaknesses

  • Free and Go plans may show ads
  • Free users get a lighter model
  • Many plans and model names can be confusing

Conclusion

There is no single winner. Claude is a focused tool for writing, coding and long documents. ChatGPT is a "Swiss Army knife" with images, voice and many extras. The best choice depends on your task — and many people use both.
